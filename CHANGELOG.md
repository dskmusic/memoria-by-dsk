# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Realism pass on every landscape: bloom, cinematic camera, weather that changes with Evolution, day / night with Light.
- Valley: golden hour, ridged mountains with snow and alpenglow, cloud shadows, forests, meadow, stream, birds, lens flare.
- Starfield: atmospheric limb, ring divisions and shadows, lightning and polar aurora, two moons, meteors, diffraction spikes.
- Ocean: waves rushing up the shore, wet sand, light path, cliff with spray, gulls, a whale, sea mist.
- Underwater: manta ray and sea turtle, bubbles, corals and anemones. Aurora: light on the snow, chimney smoke.
- Melody layer (8th layer, off in older presets): composed 8-bar themes with motif, answer and cadence, music box / celesta / flute tones.
- Two melodic moods, Fairytale and Film Score (36 new presets, 292 in total); generator grid stays even (7 x 2).
- GPU shader landscapes (toggle in Settings), FPS counter, landscape picture button, natural Neon rain, translucent jellyfish.
- Functional chord progressions (Markov chains for major and minor modes, phrases returning home) and true voice-leading in the pad.
- Quick memories: press and hold to clear. Video export without console windows, about 4x faster.
- Bass layer (follows the chord root), PWM pad with ensemble chorus, hall reverb with pre-delay, glue compressor, melodic bell phrases.
- 12 moods / 256 factory presets (Epic, Forest, Desert, Sleep added); generator keeps the best of several candidates.
- Underwater landscape (replaces Nebula); richer Ocean, Valley, Neon, Aurora and Starfield; smooth scene transitions.
- Sleep mode, tempo-synced chords, 8 quick memories with crossfades, relax mode in full screen, landscape MP4 export (ffmpeg), Help page.
- Click-free preset changes (automatic dip-and-return), smoothed delay time and layer switches.
- Living landscape engine: six self-playing layers (Drone, Pad, Texture, Shimmer, Wind, Bells) with Level, Presence, Tone and Character.
- Seed + timeline driven evolution: identical output for any host block size; DAW bounces match playback.
- Play / Stop button that also follows the DAW transport; session timer; fade-in / fade-out; Freeze.
- Harmony: 12 scales, key, octave, harmony amount (open fifths to 9th chords), automatic chord motion; 432 Hz tuning.
- MIDI: set the key, play notes on top, or both.
- Long audio export (1 min to 3 h) to WAV, FLAC or OGG, streamed to disk.
- Mood generator (Cinematic, Ambient, Meditation, Dark, Sci-Fi) with validation and level matching; mutate, blend, history.
- 136 factory presets in 8 moods (incl. Ethereal, Oceanic, Nostalgia; Cinematic Neo-Noir and Derelict); bilingual UI (automatic or manual language).
- Six landscapes (Valley, Starfield, Nebula, Neon Rain, Ocean, Aurora) with seed-dependent scenery, full-screen mode with keyboard shortcuts; maximisable Standalone with adaptive layout.
- Noise generator (pink, brown, white, ocean, vinyl); analog pad character with ensemble chorus; melodic bell phrases; click-free Freeze; quicker Stop.
